# 40-Second Demo Video Script

## 0–5 seconds

Show the Readly homepage hero, product preview, and feature cards.

Voiceover: "Still copying invoice data by hand? I build complete OCR web applications that turn documents into structured data."

## 5–10 seconds

Click **Try the demo**, show the branded access page, and continue as a guest.

Voiceover: "This production-style demo includes a polished landing page, access flow, and private OCR workspace."

## 10–18 seconds

Drag a synthetic invoice into the upload area. Show the OCR progress indicator.

Voiceover: "Upload a PDF or image and the document is processed privately in the browser."

## 18–27 seconds

Show vendor, invoice number, dates, line items, and total appearing in the review panel.

Voiceover: "The system extracts key fields, amounts, and line items while keeping every result editable."

## 27–34 seconds

Open the raw OCR view, return to extracted data, and correct one field.

Voiceover: "Review confidence, inspect the recognized text, and correct anything before export."

## 34–38 seconds

Choose Excel and click Export. Briefly show the downloaded file.

Voiceover: "Export clean JSON, CSV, or Excel data for accounting and automation."

## 38–40 seconds

Return to the full workspace.

Voiceover: "Send me your sample documents and I will design the right OCR solution for your workflow."

## Recording notes

- Use only synthetic documents without private information.
- Record at 1920 by 1080 pixels.
- Keep pointer movements slow and intentional, and make the page transitions visible.
- Add subtitles matching the voiceover.
- Do not promise perfect OCR accuracy.
